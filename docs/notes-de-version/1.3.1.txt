Réveils radio et Comptes-à-rebours :

Lancement 1mn avant l'heure en sourdine pour éviter d'être réveillé par une pub.
Montée progressive du son.

la station en cours, lecture, pause, précédent, suivant, arrêt

[EN] Alarms, countdown timers, widget, and landscape mode

Radio alarms and countdown timers:
Multiple alarms can be saved.
Playback starts silently one minute before the scheduled time to avoid being woken up by an advertisement.
Gradual volume increase.

Home screen widget:
Displays the current station; includes controls for play, pause, previous, next, and stop.
Upon opening, the app presents the last-played station or episode, ready for you to tap and start.

Podcasts:
Playback speed adjustable from 0.75× to 2×; skip back 15 seconds, skip forward 30 seconds.
Playback resumes automatically when the network connection is restored, following a network interruption lasting longer than 25 seconds.

Landscape mode:
Redesigned display layout.
