Vos favoris et vos dossiers à plusieurs niveaux, rangés depuis le téléphone

[EN] First published release: radios, podcasts, and the home server

Search the Radio Browser directory by name, country or style, and star a station
Your favourites and nested folders, filed from the phone
Background playback, current track on the lock screen, headset buttons
Plain http streams and AAC, which browsers refuse, play here
Open an export file to get back a list made elsewhere
The app stands on its own: Unicorn-TV is not required
If you have it, paired by QR code, the phone pulls its list and sends back every change, even made offline
Podcasts: search the Apple directory or paste a feed address, follow them, listen to episodes by day
Each episode resumes where you left it, shared with the PC, fifteen seconds back or forward
In French and in English, chosen from the top bar
Signed to last: the next releases will install over this one
